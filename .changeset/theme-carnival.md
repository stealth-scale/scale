theme-carnival: add the theme

- A red product with orange and yellow beside it, on navy after dark and on cream by day. The navy
  is the dark page and the light ink, the red the product and the error, the orange the secondary
  and the warning, and the yellow the accent. The dark page is written in a pale yellow.
- The four colors are stated outright and every other value is a tint or a mix of them, drawn with
  `inked`, `hues` and `scaleOf`. The specification checks the contract and the steps a reader has to
  tell apart, and leaves the contrast checks out with the reason beside the skip.
- The code family is drawn with `coded`, the keyword from the red, the string from the orange and
  the number from the yellow, and every other kind from the foundation's hues over the theme's
  pages.
