theme-dusk: add the theme

- A coral product with mauve and plum beside it, on navy after dark and on the palest coral by day,
  with soft corners. The navy is the dark page and the light ink, the plum the panel on the dark
  page and the accent by day, the mauve the secondary, and the coral the product. The dark page is
  written in a pale coral, which is the accent after dark.
- The four colors are stated outright and every other value is a tint or a mix of them, drawn with
  `inked`, `hues` and `scaleOf`. The specification checks the contract and the steps a reader has to
  tell apart, and leaves the contrast checks out with the reason beside the skip.
- The code family is drawn with `coded`, the keyword from the coral, the type from the mauve and the
  tag from the plum, and every other kind from the foundation's hues over the theme's pages.
