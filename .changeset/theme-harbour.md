theme-harbour: add the theme

- A steel blue product on navy and mist. The navy is the dark page and the light ink, the deep blue
  the panel on the dark page and the secondary by day, the steel blue the product by day and the
  secondary after dark, and the mist the light page, the dark ink and the product after dark.
- The four colors are stated outright and every other value is a tint or a mix of them, drawn with
  `inked`, `hues` and `scaleOf`. The specification checks the contract and the steps a reader has to
  tell apart, and leaves the contrast checks out with the reason beside the skip.
- The code family is drawn with `coded`, the keyword from the steel and the type from the deep blue,
  and every other kind from the foundation's hues over the theme's pages.
