theme-pine: add the theme

- A green product with teal and sage beside it, on the night after dark and on the palest sage by
  day. The night is the dark page and the light ink, the teal the secondary, the green the product,
  and the sage the dark ink, the accent and the success.
- The four colors are stated outright and every other value is a tint or a mix of them, drawn with
  `inked`, `hues` and `scaleOf`. The specification checks the contract and the steps a reader has to
  tell apart, and leaves the contrast checks out with the reason beside the skip.
